## What is Kyno?

![Kyno Preview](https://www.redsharknews.com/hubfs/Kyno%201-8.jpg)

Kyno is a professional-grade media organization platform built for video editors and production teams. It enables efficient cataloging, searching, and management of video, photo, and audio files through an intuitive and powerful interface.  

With support for over 50 video formats and codecs, Kyno allows users to import, review, and tag media effortlessly. Its robust metadata system lets you add custom information, ratings, and comments to each file. The integrated video player supports full-resolution playback and frame-accurate navigation.  

Kyno also offers:  
- **Fast Proxy Generation** – Work smoothly with high-resolution media.  
- **Deep Software Integration** – Send clips directly to Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve.  
- **Advanced Search** – Filter by metadata, duration, format, or custom tags.  
- **Project Sharing** – Export catalogs for collaboration with team members.  

Designed for performance, Kyno leverages macOS technologies like Metal acceleration and Core Storage to deliver a responsive experience even with large libraries. It is trusted by indie creators, broadcast studios, and corporate video teams alike.

## System Requirements  

- macOS 12 Monterey or newer  
- Intel or Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) processor  
- 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended for 4K media)  
- 2 GB free disk space (more required for media libraries)  
- Internet connection for activation and updates  

---

## Tips & Tricks

- Use keyword tagging to quickly find clips in large projects  
- Generate proxies for smoother playback when working with high-resolution footage  
- Customize metadata views to show the information most relevant to your workflow  
- Use the built-in video player to review clips without leaving the app
